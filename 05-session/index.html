What this surface claims. Coverage counts direct native support only (bindings × adapters/*/support.json). Fallback-only reachability is shown as a separate number and a hollow cell — a pack that reads 16/16 everywhere because you counted fallbacks is a pack whose coverage number means nothing. Set the target agent to Cursor and watch permission.request and notification grey out on the timeline: those events never fire there, so the fallback chain re-homes the sound, not the moment.

Gain staging follows §10.3 — boundary 1.0, cue 0.9, churn 0.2, churn throttled to 120 ms, polyphony capped at 6 per lane (oldest dropped, 8 ms release). Every sound renders through ×0.9 → tanh(x×1.25)×0.8 → trim → 4 ms fade, so no buffer exceeds −1.9 dBFS, and the same tanh curve sits once more across the bus because six summed voices could otherwise clip. The grep burst at t=20.4 s fires 30 tool.pre in two seconds on purpose: with the spec's defaults about half are throttled away and a tactile pack peaks at three voices. Load two bed packs into both lanes and the same burst hits the polyphony cap — which is exactly the case §10.3's lint warning is pointing at.
