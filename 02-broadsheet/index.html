From the Editors

Publish a pack once and it sounds on all three agents. Every entry below is a set of small synthesised noises bound to the moments an agent passes through — a session opening, a tool returning, a permission waiting on a human. The binding, not the noise, is the work: a pack that fires the wrong thing at the wrong volume is worse than silence.

Each entry carries its own index of bound hooks. Run the cursor down it and you will hear each hook in turn, at the gain the publisher set. Packages contain no executable code; the whole catalogue is inert JSON.
